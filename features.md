# Vibe — Feature Plan

This document describes the intended direction of Vibe. Items marked as planned are concepts, not working features in the current prototype.

## Current prototype features

- **Home feed:** displays sample posts and newly created local posts.
- **Create post:** lets a visitor publish a text post in the current browser.
- **Like interaction:** toggles a like state on a post.
- **Search:** filters visible posts by matching text.
- **Vibe World preview:** shows discovery categories.
- **Responsive navigation:** provides a compact mobile navigation bar.

## Planned signature features

### Vibe World
A discovery area for interests, creators, topics and community content.

### Vibe AI
An optional assistant that could help users write captions, find topics and discover communities. It should be transparent, privacy-conscious and designed with user control.

### Vibe Map
A map-based discovery feature for public events and community activities. Location sharing should be optional and off until a user chooses otherwise.

### Vibe Together
A space for shared activities such as group discussions, watch parties or collaborative events.

### Vibe Rewards
A positive participation system that could recognise helpful contributions. It should avoid encouraging spam or unhealthy competition.

### Vibe Communities
Topic-based groups where members can share posts and discuss common interests.

### Vibe Business
Optional public pages for small businesses and creators to share updates and information.

## Safety and privacy principles

- Give users control over what they share.
- Make account and location privacy settings clear.
- Provide reporting and blocking tools before opening a public community.
- Avoid public display of private contact details.
- Use age-appropriate defaults and moderation.
- Do not launch direct messaging or location features without proper safety design.
